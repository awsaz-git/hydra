{brand} is an AI coach for training. To coach you, it has to know things about you, some of them about your health. This page explains, in plain language, what we keep, why, who else handles it, and what you can do about it.

## Who we are

{brand} is run by {operator}. For any question about your data, write to [{email}](mailto:{email}).

## What we keep

- **Your account.** Your email address and password are handled by Clerk, our sign-in provider. We never see or store your password. In our own database we only keep an internal ID linked to your Clerk account, not your email or name.
- **Your profile**, from onboarding: your birth date, sex, height, weight, the kinds of athlete you are, and the performance numbers you choose to give (for example your best squat, or a race time). You can skip the performance questions.
- **Your preferences:** units, language and theme.
- **Your chats** with {brand}: your messages and the coach's replies. Anything you write there is kept, including health information you choose to share, such as injuries or what you eat.
- **Your consent:** which version of this policy you agreed to, and when.
- **Training plans and notes** {brand} keeps about you, once those features exist. You'll be able to see and delete them.
- **Technical data:** counters that limit how many requests an account can make in a short time (to stop abuse), and server logs with the time, the address you connected from (IP address) and the page or action requested. We don't write your password or the text of your messages to the logs.

## Why we keep it

Only to coach you: to answer your questions, build your plans in your units and at your level, and keep the service safe and working.

- We don't sell your data.
- We don't show ads.
- We don't use your data to train AI models.

Because some of this is health information, we only use it with your explicit consent, which you give in onboarding by ticking a box. You can withdraw it by deleting your account.

## Who else handles your data

- **Clerk** (United States): sign-in, email codes and account security, including a bot check run by Cloudflare.
- **DeepSeek** (**China**): the AI that writes the coach's replies.
- **Neon** (Frankfurt, Germany, EU): our database.

**What DeepSeek receives:** the coach's instructions, the recent messages of the chat you're in, and your new message. It doesn't receive your email or your account ID. DeepSeek processes this under its own privacy policy, on servers in China, where data protection law differs from the EU's.

The servers that run the app itself will be named here once its hosting is final.

## How long we keep it

- **Chats, profile, preferences and plans:** until you delete them, or your account.
- **Usage and cost records** (how much AI work your requests needed): 12 months.
- **Your Clerk account:** until your account is deleted.

## Your rights

You can:

- **See** your data: your chats in the app, your profile and preferences in their pages as they're added.
- **Correct** it: change your profile and preferences.
- **Delete** it: delete any chat now. Deleting your whole account, which deletes everything, is coming to the settings page; until then, write to us and we'll do it.
- **Get a copy** of it: write to us.

Write to [{email}](mailto:{email}) for any of these, or if you think we got something wrong.

## How we protect it

- The connections to the database, to Clerk and to the AI are encrypted. The connection between your browser and the app will be too (HTTPS) once the app is public.
- Every request is checked against your sign-in, and the database itself only lets each request reach the data of the account that made it.
- The app's access to the database is limited to reading and writing records; it can't change or delete the database's structure.
- Passwords are handled by Clerk, never by us.

## Age

{brand} is for people aged 16 and over. You tell us your birth date in onboarding; if you're under 16, you can't continue.

## Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters for you, we'll update the date at the top and ask for your consent again before you continue.
